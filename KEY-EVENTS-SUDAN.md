# Key events — Sudan

Same criterion as Ukraine: an event earns a place if it CHANGED THE STATUS of
the location, or is historically significant in its own right regardless of
military effect.

Every date verified against the cited source, 31 Aug 2026. [CONTESTED] marks
a date or figure where sources genuinely disagree — the note says how.

A warning specific to this conflict: the death tolls here are not merely
uncertain, they are irreconcilable. El Fasher ranges from ~6,000 (UN, first
three days) to ~60,000 (Yale, three weeks). Where that is true the line
carries a range with attribution and never a single number.

# Notes on what is NOT here

## No single "recapture of Omdurman"
There was never one announcement. The two defensible anchors are the radio and
television complex on 12 March 2024 and Salha on 20 May 2025, and both are
listed as separate events rather than collapsed into a false single date.

## Death tolls
El Fasher and Ardamata both have ranges that differ by an order of magnitude
between credible sources. Ardamata (Nov 2023, ~800 to over 2,000 killed) is not
listed above because it happened at Geneina, which has no marker. If you add a
Geneina marker it belongs there.

## The famine declarations are two, not one
1 August 2024 confirmed famine in Zamzam camp. 24 December 2024 confirmed it in
at least five areas including Abu Shouk, Al Salam and the western Nuba
Mountains. They are frequently conflated. Only the first is listed, under
El Fasher, because that is where its marker sits.
