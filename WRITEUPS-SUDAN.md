## CONFLICT: Sudan civil war

Fighting broke out on 15 April 2023 between Sudan's army and the Rapid Support Forces, a paramilitary group that had grown out of the Darfur militias, after a power struggle between their two commanders over the country's political transition turned into open war. The army holds the centre and east, including the recaptured capital; the RSF holds most of Darfur and runs a rival administration from Nyala. The war has produced the largest displacement crisis in the world and confirmed famine, and no peace process is under way.

---

## Khartoum

Khartoum is where the war began: on 15 April 2023 fighting erupted across the capital and the Rapid Support Forces held most of it within days, including the airport and much of the city centre. The army fought its way back over the following two years, retaking the Republican Palace on 21 March 2025 and clearing the last RSF positions from Khartoum state that May. The government returned from Port Sudan on 11 January 2026 to a capital largely destroyed.

## Omdurman

Omdurman sits across the Nile from Khartoum and was divided between the two forces for most of the war. The army recaptured the national radio and television complex there on 12 March 2024, its first significant gain in the capital region, but fighting continued in the southern districts for another year. Salha, the last RSF stronghold, fell on 20 May 2025, and Khartoum state was declared clear of the RSF the same day.

## El Fasher

El Fasher was the last city in Darfur still held by the army, and the RSF besieged it from May 2024. The siege lasted eighteen months, starved the displacement camps around the city and ended when the RSF stormed the army's divisional headquarters on 26 October 2025. Satellite imagery and UN investigators documented mass killings in the days that followed, and a UN fact-finding mission concluded in February 2026 that the events bore the hallmarks of genocide against the Fur and Zaghawa.

## Nyala

Nyala is the largest city in Darfur and fell to the RSF on 26 October 2023, handing them the region's main airport and its principal supply hub. It has since become the seat of the parallel government that the RSF and its allies established, with Hemedti sworn in as chairman of its presidential council there on 30 August 2025. That administration holds no international recognition and represents the clearest step yet towards Sudan's partition.

## Port Sudan

Port Sudan on the Red Sea became the wartime capital after the government fled Khartoum in 2023, and it handles most of the aid entering the country. It went untouched until 4 May 2025, when RSF drones struck the airbase, the fuel depots and the power station over several consecutive days, ending the assumption that the east lay beyond the war's reach. The government returned to Khartoum in January 2026, but the port remains the country's lifeline.
