# Key events — Gaza

Same criterion as Ukraine and Sudan: an event earns a place if it CHANGED THE
STATUS of the location, or is historically significant in its own right.

Every date verified against the cited source, 6 Sep 2026.

Two warnings specific to this conflict:

1. DEATH TOLLS. The cumulative total and the post-ceasefire count are NOT
   additive — the cumulative figure rises partly because bodies are recovered
   from rubble and added retroactively. A Lancet Global Health mortality survey
   found roughly 35% MORE violent deaths than the health ministry recorded for
   the same period, so the ministry's figures read as a floor, not an overcount.

2. THE YELLOW LINE MOVES. Israeli control behind it was ~53% at establishment
   in Oct 2025, 64.9% by June 2026 (OCHA), with Netanyahu directing expansion to
   70% in May 2026. Never cite that percentage without a date attached.

# Editorial notes

## The ceasefire has three dates, not one
Announced 8 October 2025, signed in Egypt 9 October, in effect 10 October after
Israeli cabinet approval. A separate declaratory document was signed at Sharm
el-Sheikh on 13 October. The write-up uses 10 October, the date it took effect.
Sources conflate all four.

## Nothing listed for Jabalia or Khan Younis in 2026
Deliberate. The research found no status-changing event at either in 2026 beyond
incremental movement of the line and individual strikes. An empty stretch is
more honest than padding it.

## Death tolls not placed on any marker
67,183 killed and 169,841 injured as of the ceasefire (Gaza health ministry via
OCHA, 9 Oct 2025); 1,123 killed since, as of 13 July 2026. These belong to the
conflict, not to any one location, and putting them on a marker would imply they
happened there. If a conflict-level events layer is built, they go there.
